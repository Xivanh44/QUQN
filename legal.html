1. What QUQN is

QUQN is a community/meme token deployed using the BRC-20 protocol on Bitcoin mainnet. Max supply: 21,000,000 QUQN. Mint limit: 1,000 QUQN per mint. QUQN does not represent equity, debt, a claim on assets, redemption rights, yield, dividends or guaranteed utility.

2. Fair-mint model

The project is intended to operate as a public fair mint. There is no presale, ICO or primary token sale by the project. The project does not receive payment, fees, commissions, personal data or other monetary or non-monetary benefits from prospective holders in exchange for QUQN. Users may independently incur Bitcoin network fees and third-party service fees when using external tools. Those fees are not received by QUQN.

6. Technical risks

Risks include network congestion, high or wasted fees, transaction mistakes, incompatible wallets, lost private keys, software or indexer errors, protocol changes, third-party outages and differences between services.

9. MiCA / European Union note

Article 4(3)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA) states that Title II does not apply to offers to the public of certain crypto-assets where the crypto-asset is offered for free. The same provision says a crypto-asset is not considered offered for free where purchasers must provide personal data to the offeror in exchange for it, or where the offeror receives fees, commissions, or monetary or non-monetary benefits from prospective holders in exchange for it.

Article 4(4) also states that the exemptions in paragraphs 2 and 3 do not apply where the offeror, or a person acting on its behalf, makes known an intention to seek admission to trading. QUQN is therefore deliberately described by the factual characteristics of its current fair-mint setup; this website does not claim that a regulator or court has determined QUQN to be exempt.

Official references: ESMA — MiCA Article 4 · EUR-Lex — Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 · AMF — public offerings and admission to trading.
