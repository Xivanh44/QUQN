What is QUQN?

QUQN is a community-driven BRC-20 meme coin on Bitcoin mainnet, built around one small coq with completely unreasonable crypto ambitions.

QUQN launched as a fair mint with no presale, no ICO and no creator mint fee. The maximum supply is 21,000,000 QUQN and each mint creates 1,000 QUQN. The project mixes Bitcoin and BRC-20 culture with memes, a playful community and the motto Small Coq. Big Dreams.

If you are new to Bitcoin inscriptions or BRC-20 tokens, the beginner guide explains how to verify the ticker, understand the fees and mint QUQN through compatible third-party services such as UniSat or OKX Wallet.

Yes. QUQN is experimental and highly speculative. It can have no market or value.

What is a BRC-20 fair mint?

A BRC-20 fair mint lets participants mint tokens under the same public rules until the maximum supply is reached. For QUQN, there was no presale or ICO and the project does not receive a creator mint fee.

How do I mint QUQN?

Use the QUQN beginner guide to verify the Bitcoin network, ticker QUQN, the 21,000,000 maximum supply and the 1,000 QUQN mint limit, then choose a compatible third-party BRC-20 inscription service such as UniSat or OKX Wallet.
