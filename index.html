FAQ

Before the coq gets carried away.

Is QUQN sold by this website or the project?

No. The website does not sell QUQN, connect to wallets, custody funds or receive payments for the token.

Where are the technical details?

Can QUQN go to zero?

Yes. QUQN is experimental and highly speculative. It can have no market or value.

What is a BRC-20 fair mint?

A BRC-20 fair mint lets participants mint tokens under the same public rules until the maximum supply is reached. For QUQN, there was no presale or ICO and the project does not receive a creator mint fee.

How do I mint QUQN?

Use the QUQN beginner guide to verify the Bitcoin network, ticker QUQN, the 21,000,000 maximum supply and the 1,000 QUQN mint limit, then choose a compatible third-party BRC-20 inscription service such as UniSat or OKX Wallet.

Choose your mint route

QUQN is a standard 4-byte BRC-20 on Bitcoin. You can mint it through a compatible third-party BRC-20 inscription service. UniSat is the recommended route, and OKX Wallet is an alternative.

At 1,000 QUQN per full mint, that corresponds to1,000 QUQN

Option 1 — UniSat

RECOMMENDED
  1. Open the UniSat BRC-20 inscription page.
  2. Search for ticker QUQN and open it.
  3. Select Mint and confirm it is the 4-byte (classic) BRC-20.
  4. Verify 1,000 QUQN for a full mint and choose the number of mints.
  5. Check the receiving Bitcoin address and all network/service fees.
  6. Confirm the order and wait for Bitcoin confirmation.
Open UniSat BRC-20 mint ↗UniSat guide ↗

Option 2 — OKX Wallet

  1. Open OKX Wallet and go to More → NFT → Inscriptions → Inscription Center.
  2. Select the Bitcoin/BRC-20 inscription route, then Mint.
  3. Enter ticker QUQN.
  4. Enter 1,000 QUQN for a full mint and choose the number of mints.
  5. Enter or verify the recipient Bitcoin address.
  6. Review the mint summary and fees, then confirm.
Official OKX BRC-20 guide ↗

Important: always verify Bitcoin, ticker QUQN, maximum supply 21,000,000 and the official UniScan page before minting. Third-party interfaces and fees can change.
